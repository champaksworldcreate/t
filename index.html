Programming Commands

LAND brings the drone down.

DOWN 10 moves the drone down.

FORWARD 15 moves the drone forward.

BACK 15 moves the drone backward.

LEFT 20 moves the drone left.

RIGHT 20 moves the drone right.

ROTATE 45 rotates the drone.

PHOTO captures a camera flash.

WAIT 1000 waits for 1000 milliseconds.

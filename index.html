Step-based plot finder

These filters are intentionally realistic: location, intended use, price range, plot size, corner plot, minimum road width, and possession preference. All questions and choices below actually affect the results.

Buyer journey

Jump to any step. Every answer updates the map, cards, and WhatsApp enquiry text.

Step 1 — Where do you want to search?

Search a city, locality, landmark, or area name. This uses OpenStreetMap geocoding through Nominatim. Matching projects are ranked by proximity to your searched point.

Step 2 — What is your main purpose for buying?

This changes which projects appear first. Commercial buyers, for example, should not see the same shortlist as family-home buyers.

Step 3 — What is your comfortable budget range?

Use a realistic minimum and maximum. Results outside the range are excluded.

Step 4 — What plot size are you looking for?

Smaller plots suit tighter budgets, medium plots fit most homes, and larger plots support bigger houses or better frontage.

Step 5 — Do you specifically want a corner plot?

Corner plots often carry a premium because of better visibility, extra openness, and road access.

Step 6 — What is the minimum road width you want?

Buyers often care about frontage quality and accessibility. This filter removes plots below your chosen road width threshold.

How it works

INPUT gives 0 or 1.
AND outputs 1 only if all inputs are 1.
OR outputs 1 if at least one input is 1.
XOR outputs 1 if inputs differ.
NOT flips 0 to 1 and 1 to 0.
NAND, NOR, XNOR are inverted forms.
OUTPUT displays the final signal.

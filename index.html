Talking Avatar Demo

This is a demo version of a floating speaking face that can later be merged into your PPSpeak system. It blinks, animates the mouth while speaking, and highlights the current paragraph.

Demo Speak Paragraphs

Welcome to Programmer's Picnic. This is the introduction paragraph. In this demo, the avatar will start speaking, the mouth will animate, and the active paragraph will be highlighted.
The main idea is simple. We use browser speech synthesis for narration, and while the narration runs, the avatar enters speaking mode. This gives a lively teaching experience.
Later, we can replace this demo face with your own photo based avatar, or even build a more advanced version that looks like a real speaking presenter.

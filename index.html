Share Trading Profit Optimizer

This pure HTML, CSS and JavaScript app uses dummy data to simulate a strategy where you invest when the share price falls and sell when it rises above your average cost. You can change the buy amount, dip trigger, sell target, scaling rule and number of buys, then compare the results and run a simple optimizer.

How to think about maximizing profit

The goal is not just more profit, but better risk-adjusted profit.

Key variables to optimize
  • At what percentage drop from the recent peak should the first buy happen?
  • How much money should be invested in each buy?
  • How much should later buys be scaled up?
  • At what percentage above average cost should the system sell?
  • How many buys should be allowed in one down cycle?
Important caution
A strategy can look excellent on past data and then fail in future data. Real optimization should use a training period and then a separate testing period.
